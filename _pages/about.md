We are an open discussion forum of people interested in ethical and responsible approaches to research and development of technologies, especially in the area of artificial intelligence. Our aim is to educate ourselves through a series of debates and discussions on interdisciplinary topics, such as professional ethics, AI safety, and regulation.
Recognizing that many of these topics are outside our technological expertise, we are trying to maximize our exposure to experts from other domains interacting with AI, e.g., philosophy, ethics, and social sciences.

## Seminars
Currently, we organize irregular public events at the premises of Czech Technical University, Faculty of Electrical Engineering, at Charles Square 13 in Prague. If you are interested, get in touch with any of our members.
