We are excited to introduce our Responsible AI Group, a community of researchers, developers, and students located at Prague dedicated to exploring the ethical dimensions and responsible approaches to artificial intelligence development. Our group serves as an open forum for people interested in ethical and responsible approaches to research and development of technologies, with a special focus on artificial intelligence. We consist of members with diverse backgrounds and perspectives, united by a common interest in ensuring that AI technologies are developed and deployed in ways that benefit society while minimizing potential harms.

## Our Mission

Our primary aim is to educate ourselves through a series of debates and discussions on interdisciplinary topics related to AI development, including:

- Professional ethics in technology development
- AI safety principles and practices
- Regulatory frameworks for AI
- Fairness and bias in algorithmic systems
- Environmental impacts of AI technologies
- Privacy and security concerns
- Philosophical questions around AI and human values

Recognizing that many of these topics extend beyond our technological expertise, we actively seek to engage with experts from other domains interacting with AI, such as philosophy, ethics, social sciences, and policy.

## Our Activities

### Regular Seminars

We organize public events at the premises of Czech Technical University, Faculty of Electrical Engineering, at Charles Square 13 in Prague. These seminars typically feature:

- Presentations on key topics in responsible AI
- Panel discussions with experts from various disciplines
- Workshops and practical sessions
- Open debates on current AI developments and their implications

### Community Building

Beyond our formal events, we work to build a community of practice around responsible AI development. We believe that ethical considerations should be integral to AI research and development, not merely an afterthought.

## Our Approach to Responsible AI

We view Responsible AI (RAI) as an umbrella term encompassing multiple dimensions:

1. **Ethics and Fairness** - Ensuring AI systems don't perpetuate or amplify societal biases and injustices
2. **Safety and Alignment** - Working to ensure AI systems behave as intended and align with human values
3. **Governance** - Exploring appropriate regulatory frameworks and institutional oversight
4. **Environmental Impact** - Considering the resource consumption and environmental footprint of AI
5. **Security and Privacy** - Protecting sensitive data and ensuring systems are resistant to attacks
6. **Trustworthiness** - Building systems that are transparent, explainable, and deserving of human trust
7. **Philosophy and Value Systems** - Examining deeper questions about consciousness, agency, and values

Rather than seeing these as competing concerns, we believe in a holistic approach that recognizes the interconnected nature of these dimensions. While different stakeholders may prioritize different aspects, all are necessary components of truly responsible AI development.
